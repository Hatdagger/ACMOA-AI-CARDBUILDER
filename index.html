Build an AC, give it an emblem and paint, and save it as one square image. The whole build travels in a colour bar down the left edge, the way a printer's test strip carries its patches: first the emblem's sixteen game colours, then only as many blocks as the build needs.

Human PLUS is all or nothing here. With it on, the game fields the AC even when it is over its weight or energy limits.

These are the game's own checks. Leg load is everything the legs carry. Arm load is the arms plus the two hand weapons (weapon arms alone) against the core's limit. Some parts can't be fitted together, as in the game's garage: caterpillars take no booster and drop most radars, WG-1-KARASAWA needs steady legs, ANKS-1A46J carries no back weapons, and the big back units take both back slots. Energy drain is every part against the generator's output.
